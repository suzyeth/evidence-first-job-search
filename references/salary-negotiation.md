# 谈薪手册

> 收到 offer 时先冷静读这份,不要在情绪里做决定。
> **永远不在第一通电话里当场接受。** 要 24–48 小时考虑期是行业常规。
> 薪资区间和法律细节以英国为例;在别的国家用之前,换成当地数据并核实当地法规。

---

## 0. 心态

| ❌ 焦虑驱动的想法 | ✅ 正确想法 |
|------------------|------------|
| "他们给的数就是最终数" | 第一份 offer 通常留有 5–15% 的空间,招聘方预期你会谈 |
| "谈了会显得贪心" | 中级岗谈薪是常态,不谈反而显得你不看重自己 |
| "谈了 offer 可能就没了" | 合理谈判不会让 offer 被撤回,只有离谱要求才会 |
| "只有工资能谈" | 工资只是其中一项:签约奖金、入职日、假期、股权、远程天数都能谈 |
| "我需要对方担保签证,不能挑" | 担保是对方主动在你身上投资;你的签证压力不是让步的理由 |

## 1. 先做功课:知道这个岗位的薪资区间

**数据来源**:Glassdoor、Levels.fyi、LinkedIn Salary、行业薪资报告、同行私下打听、JD 里公开的区间。

### 参考:英国游戏行业基本工资区间(2025–2026 粗略值)

| 级别 | 设计线(£) | 程序线(£) |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Junior / Associate | 28–38k | 30–45k |
| Mid | 40–55k | 45–65k |
| Senior | 55–75k | 65–90k |
| Lead | 70–95k | 85–115k |
| Principal / Director | 90–130k+ | 110–150k+ |

**公司类型修正**:
- 大厂 / 上市公司:区间上浮 10–20%
- 中型工作室:标准区间
- 独立 / 小工作室:下浮 10–15%,但可能有股权或分红
- AI / 大型科技公司:按科技行业区间,明显高于游戏行业

→ 在 `applications/<slug>/` 里给每个进入 offer 阶段的岗位写一行**估计区间 + 数据来源**。

## 2. 收到 offer 后的流程

### Phase A — 接到 offer 的那通电话 / 邮件

招聘方会问:"Are you ready to accept?" **不要当场接受**,这样回:

> "Thank you so much — I'm genuinely excited about this. I'd like to take 24–48 hours to review the full offer. Could you send the written offer with the breakdown (salary, bonus, benefits, start date, holiday)?"

→ **先要书面 offer**,它是谈判的基础。

### Phase B — 收到书面 offer,逐项看

- 基本工资
- 签约奖金
- 绩效 / 年终奖结构
- 股权 / RSU
- 年假(英国法定最少 28 天,含公共假日;很多公司是 25 + 8)
- 养老金雇主匹配比例
- 私人医疗保险
- 通知期(双向)
- 试用期(通常 3–6 个月,期间通知期常缩短)
- 签证担保年限(如需要)
- 入职日期是否灵活
- 远程 / 混合办公政策(是否写进合同)

### Phase C — 决定要不要谈

```
offer 金额在区间里的位置
│
├── 高于 75 分位 → 可以直接接受
├── 50–75 分位   → 可以争取 5–10%
└── 低于 50 分位 → 必须谈,或者放弃
```

谈之前心里先定好三个数:**目标数**(比 offer 高 5–15%)、**开口数**(比目标再高约 10%,留出空间)、**底线数**(低于它就拒)。

## 3. 谈判邮件模板

### 模板 1 — 还价(主谈基本工资)

**Subject**:`Re: Offer — [Role] — [Your Name]`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thank you again for the offer — I'm genuinely excited about
[SPECIFIC ASPECT: the team / mission / project].

I've had time to review and want to discuss the compensation.
Based on my research of comparable roles in the [market/field],
and given my experience — [2–3 strongest, verifiable points from
your master CV] — I was hoping we could land closer to
£[TARGET] base.

I understand budgets aren't infinite. If there's flexibility
there, I'd be ready to confirm acceptance promptly. If not,
would there be room on signing bonus, start date, or holiday
allocation?

Looking forward to your response.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

关键措辞:
- **"based on my research"**:表明你做过功课,不是随口要价
- **"comparable roles"**:对方不用透露内部数据也能回应
- **"ready to confirm acceptance promptly"**:给对方一个"能成交"的信号
- **"if not, would there be room on…"**:始终给对方一条能答应的路
- 你的经历要点**必须来自 master-cv-data.md**,并遵守保密项

### 模板 2 — 手上有多个流程

**Subject**:`Quick question on timeline — [Role]`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

I wanted to be transparent. I'm in a late-stage process with
another company and expect a decision request from them within
[N] working days. [Company] is my preferred option, but I want
to give both teams a clean answer.

If there's flexibility to expedite the offer / final round for
this role, that would help me commit cleanly. Happy to discuss.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ **不报对方的具体金额。** 对方知道你有竞争力,自己会往高处报。
→ **不撒谎。** 没有其他流程就不要发这封,穿帮代价很高。

### 模板 3 — 基本工资被拒后,换维度谈

**Subject**:`Re: [Role] offer follow-up`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thanks for the response and for clarifying the constraints on base.

Could we look at structuring this differently:
- £[X] base (your offer)
- Plus £[Y] signing bonus to bridge to my target
- And [extra holiday / earlier review / extra remote day]?

This way the budget reality holds but the package lands closer
to where I'd be confident closing. Open to your suggestion.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ 基本工资常被职级区间卡死,但签约奖金、假期、入职日往往能动。**让对方告诉你哪一项有空间。**

## 4. 工资以外能谈的条件

| 项目 | 谈成概率 | 怎么提 |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 签约奖金 | 中–高 | "to bridge the gap" |
| 入职日期 | 高 | "I'd like to start on [date]" |
| 年假 | 中 | 多争取 2–5 天 |
| 远程天数 | 中 | "would you consider 3 office / 2 remote?" |
| 签证担保年限 | 中–高 | 见下 |
| 股权 / RSU | 看公司类型 | 大厂通常会主动提;小公司谨慎问 |
| 试用期 | 低–中 | 6 个月 → 3 个月,偶尔能谈 |
| 首次绩效评估 | 中 | 从 12 个月提前到 6 个月 |

### 需要签证担保时:担保年限是隐性筹码

以英国 Skilled Worker 为例,担保证书(CoS)年限越长,你需要续签的次数越少,每次续签都有费用、时间和风险。
→ 在 offer 阶段主动问:"Could the Certificate of Sponsorship be for the maximum term?"
→ 少续签一两次省下的钱和麻烦,可能比多谈几千英镑基本工资更有价值。具体年限和规则以当时的官方移民政策为准。

## 5. 不要说的话

| ❌ 不说 | 为什么 |
|---------|------|
| "I need this offer for my visa" | 显得急迫,对方会压低报价 |
| 主动报出现在的工资 | 你的价值不等于上家给多少。英国法律并不禁止对方询问,但你可以不答,把话题引回岗位区间 |
| "I have an offer from [Company] at £[X]" | 跨公司报具体数字不专业,也给对方反向锚定的把柄 |
| "I'll take whatever you offer" | 等于自己降薪 5–15% |
| "I really need this job" | 急迫信号 |
| "Can we sign today?" | 不给自己留思考时间 |
| 抱怨现雇主或前同事 | 永远减分 |

## 6. 多个 offer 的时间协调

**同一周拿到多个 offer**:拿到第一个后,马上告诉其他还在面的公司(不报金额),问能否加快。很多公司能把流程提前一两周。

**第一个 offer 早,第二个晚三四周**:第一个 offer 通常给一周期限,可以请求延长:
> "I'm finalising consideration with another late-stage process. Could the deadline be extended to [DATE]?"

延一周通常能同意,两周以上对方多半会拒。

**只有一个 offer**:不要虚构"另一个 offer"。照样可以谈(用模板 1 的 "comparable roles" 说法),只是筹码小一些。

## 7. 接受 offer

### Step 1 — 用邮件确认,不只靠电话

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thank you — I'm pleased to accept the offer for [Role] on the
agreed terms: £[X] base, [signing bonus], starting [date]
[, with sponsorship for the agreed term].

I'll wait for the formal offer letter and contract.
Excited to join the team.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ 把所有口头谈定的条件写进邮件,让对方回邮件确认。这是你手里的书面记录。

### Step 2 — 仔细看合同(必要时找劳动法律师)

offer letter 不等于合同。合同来了重点看:
- 通知期是否对等
- 竞业条款(地域、时长)
- 知识产权归属(你的个人项目算不算公司的?)
- 签约奖金回收条款(一年内离职要不要退)
- 解雇条件

花一小时找劳动法律师咨询,通常值得。

### Step 3 — 向现雇主辞职

**只在新合同签好(需要签证的,担保也已出具)之后**再提辞职。辞职信简短、礼貌,不讲原因,不抱怨:

```
Dear [Manager],

I'm writing to formally resign from my position as [Title],
effective [DATE — per notice period].

I'm grateful for the opportunity to have worked on [project]
over the past [N] months. I'll do everything I can to ensure
a smooth handover during my notice period.

Thank you and best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 8. 对话速查(谈之前过一遍)

```
对方:"What are your salary expectations?"
你:  "I'd love to first hear the band for this role — I want to make
      sure my expectations are calibrated to where this level sits
      at [Company]."
→ 让对方先报数
```

```
对方:"We can offer £45k."
你:  "Thank you. Based on my research and experience — [2–3 strongest
      verifiable points] — I was hoping we could land closer to £[开口数].
      Is there flexibility?"
→ 开口比目标高约 10%,留出空间
```

```
对方:"The max we can do is £48k."
你:  "Could we look at a signing bonus, extra holiday, or an earlier
      review to bridge the gap?"
→ 换维度继续谈
```

```
对方:"OK — £48k plus £3k signing."
你:  "That works. Could you send the formal offer letter with these terms?"
→ 口头谈定的条件立刻要书面确认
```
