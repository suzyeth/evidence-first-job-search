# 投递后跟进邮件

> 投递后各阶段的跟进模板。**每封控制在 100 词以内**:招聘官没耐心读长邮件。
> 模板里的 `[Your Name]` / `[Portfolio]` 等占位符在发送前替换。

---

## 通用原则

1. **不催。** 不写 "still waiting" / "any update" 这类话。
2. **每次带一点新东西。** 新项目、新文章、新成果,给对方一个回复的理由。
3. **语言跟着投递语言走。** 英文投的英文跟进,中文投的中文跟进。
4. **≤ 100 词。** 写完数一下。
5. **标题 5–7 个词,写清岗位。** 招聘官一天收几十封,"Hello" / "Following up" 这类泛标题容易被忽略或进垃圾箱。

---

## 模板 1 — 投递 2 周无回应(标准跟进)

**何时用**:投出 14 天没有任何邮件。

**Subject**:`Following up on [Role] application — [Your Name]`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name or "Hiring Team"],

Just following up on my application for the [Role Title] role
submitted [date]. I wanted to flag a recent project that may be
relevant — [1 sentence: something new you shipped / wrote].

If timing isn't right or the role has been filled, no problem;
I'd just appreciate knowing where things stand so I can plan
accordingly.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

→ "so I can plan accordingly" 是关键句:让对方意识到有人在等,不回复是有成本的。

---

## 模板 2 — 投递 4 周无回应(主动收尾,可选)

**何时用**:模板 1 之后又过了 2 周(投出共 28 天)。

**Subject**:`Closing the loop — [Role] — [Your Name]`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Wanted to close the loop on my application for [Role] from
[date]. As I haven't heard back, I'm assuming the role has moved
forward without me — totally understand.

If a similar role opens at [Company] in the future, I'd be keen
to hear about it. In the meantime, my work is at [Portfolio URL].

All the best,
[Your Name]
```

→ 主动收尾有时反而能换来回复;就算没有,你也可以放下这家,把精力投到别处。

---

## 模板 3 — 电话初筛后 24 小时内(感谢信)

**何时用**:每次初筛后 24 小时内发,没有例外。

**Subject**:`Thank you — [Role] conversation`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thanks for the time today. The conversation about [SPECIFIC
THING DISCUSSED — e.g. "how the team splits live content and
new features"] was clarifying.

[OPTIONAL: follow up on something they said — "You mentioned
the team uses [X] — I'd love to read more if there's a public
talk or post."]

Looking forward to next steps.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ 必须**具体**。只写 "thank you for the call" 等于告诉对方你没在听。引用一件对方说过的具体的事。

---

## 模板 4 — 用人经理面试后 24 小时内(加强版感谢信)

**何时用**:用人经理面试或更后面的轮次。

**Subject**:`Thank you — [Role] interview`

```
Hi [HM Name],

Thanks for the time today. Two things stuck with me:

1. [SPECIFIC THING from the interview — a framing or idea of
theirs that resonated, and why]

2. [A question you thought about afterwards, or an idea you
wanted to add — e.g. "On the problem you raised about X, I've
been thinking that Y might work because Z."]

If helpful, more of my thinking is at [Portfolio URL].

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ 第 2 条是真正拉开差距的地方。大部分候选人只发 "thanks";第 2 条说明面试结束后你还在想这个问题。

---

## 模板 5 — 收到拒信(维护关系)

**何时用**:被明确拒绝,不管是自动拒信还是个人邮件。

**Subject**:`Re: [Role] decision — thank you`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thanks for letting me know, and for the time you took
considering my application. I appreciate the response.

If a similar role at [Company] opens in the future, I'd be
glad to hear about it — happy to be considered.

[OPTIONAL: Any feedback on my application would be valuable.]

Wishing you and the team well.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ **被拒也回。** 招聘官和经理会跳槽,这次拒你的人半年后可能在另一家公司招人,他们会记得谁有礼貌。

---

## 模板 6 — 收到 offer(谈判的开场)

**何时用**:收到 offer,需要时间考虑或想谈条件。详细打法见 [salary-negotiation.md](salary-negotiation.md)。

**Subject**:`Re: Offer — [Role] — [Your Name]`

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thank you for the offer — I'm genuinely excited about [SPECIFIC
ASPECT — team / mission / project].

Before formally responding, I'd like to discuss two points:

1. Compensation: could you share the band this role sits in?
I want to make sure we're calibrated.

2. Start date: I have a [N]-week notice period with my current
employer. Could we target [DATE]?

Happy to schedule a call if easier. Thanks for your patience.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

→ **不要当场接受。** 24–48 小时考虑期是行业常规。

## 标题库

| 阶段 | 好的标题 | 差的标题 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 投递 2 周后 | `Following up on [Role] application — [Your Name]` | `Hello` |
| 初筛后 | `Thank you — [Role] conversation` | `Re: Phone screen` |
| 面试后 | `Thank you — [Role] interview` | `Following up` |
| 被拒后 | `Re: [Role] decision — thank you` | `Acknowledged` |
| Offer 谈判 | `Re: Offer — [Role] — [Your Name]` | `Considering` |
| 主动收尾 | `Closing the loop — [Role] — [Your Name]` | `Final follow-up` |

## 节奏

```
Day 0:   投递
Day 1:   收到 ATS 自动回复(没收到先查垃圾箱,但仍按下面走)
Day 7:   什么都不做(给对方时间)
Day 14:  模板 1
Day 21:  仍无回应 → 不再跟进,在 applications.md 降为观察
Day 28:  模板 2(可选)
```

面试阶段:

```
初筛后 24h:         模板 3
用人经理面试后 24h:  模板 4
提交 take-home 后:   等 7 天再跟进(见 take-home-playbook.md)
终面后 24h:         模板 4 变体
收到 offer:         模板 6
```

## 招聘官 vs 用人经理

**招聘官(Talent / HR)**:关心流程信息(通知期、薪资、工作许可、入职日期)。跟进可以偏事务性,标准感谢信就够了。

**用人经理(未来的上级)**:关心你怎么思考问题。跟进必须有实质内容(模板 4 第 2 条)。这是你未来一两年要共事的人,印象会持续。

→ 给用人经理的感谢信是面试的延续,不是收尾。

**命中率最高的公式**:感谢 + 一点新想法或新证据 + 一处对谈话内容的具体引用。不堆客套话。
